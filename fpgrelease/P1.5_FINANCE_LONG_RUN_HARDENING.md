# FPG P1.5 — Finance Long-Run Hardening

## Findings

The season-end financial movement existed in two places:

- `FinanceEngine.processSeason()`
- `WorldEngine._applySeasonFinancialMovement()`

Both added club income, so a normal season could receive two independent season-end cash injections. That made long-run economy artificially inflationary and made balance tests misleading.

## Changes

- `FinanceEngine` is now the single owner of season-end cashflow.
- The duplicate `WorldEngine._applySeasonFinancialMovement()` path was removed.
- Season-end cashflow is composed once from season income, prize/reputation income and financial penalty.
- Added `test/finance_long_run_regression_test.dart` covering exact one-pass accounting and 10 repeated season cycles.
