## What is now different on the pitch
- A high-press/wing-play team has more aggressive full-back occupation.
- Possession teams can use a playmaker and false nine shape.
- Counter/direct teams create more direct forward profiles.
- Low-block teams retain an anchor and deeper defensive protection.
- Wide players can stay wide or move inside depending on style.

## Remaining major gameplay gap
The current engine still uses the league result as the official outcome and schedules its presentation. A later controlled migration is required before the gameplay simulation itself becomes the sole generator of match results.
