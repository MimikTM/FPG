# Phase 85 — Content & Balance Pass

Status: **CONTENT AUDIT READY / PLAYTEST REQUIRED**

The existing systems already cover career progression, training, matches, transfers, contracts, relationships, media/news, finance, reputation, youth, international play and season progression.

## Content acceptance targets
- Every career day should expose at least one meaningful decision or football activity when applicable.
- Repeated event families must support multiple outcomes rather than a single deterministic reward.
- Transfer and contract choices must remain meaningful at low, mid and high reputation.
- Match difficulty must not make one position or play style universally optimal.
- Long-run career must retain progression pressure after the initial breakout season.

## Balance test matrix
- [ ] low OVR starter
- [ ] average OVR starter
- [ ] high OVR star
- [ ] weak club
- [ ] title contender
- [ ] relegation fight
- [ ] successful transfer
- [ ] failed negotiation
- [ ] injury-heavy period
- [ ] 3+ consecutive matches
- [ ] 2+ seasons
- [ ] 10-season integrity run

## Exit criterion
No single strategy should dominate the career loop, and a player should have meaningful decisions at every career stage.
