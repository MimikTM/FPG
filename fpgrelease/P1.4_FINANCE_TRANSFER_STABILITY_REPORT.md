# FPG P1.4 — Finance & Transfer Stability

## Changes
- Transfer AI now evaluates transfer fee + one year of wages together.
- Clubs keep an operating reserve (20% of budget, with a minimum floor).
- Negotiated transfers are blocked when the initial fee plus first-year wage cost would consume the reserve.
- Added a regression test for transfer affordability.

## Goal
Prevent long-run economic collapse caused by clubs repeatedly spending nearly their entire budget on transfers and wages.
